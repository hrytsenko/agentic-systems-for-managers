## Principles

The guide is driven by the need to judge cost and risk: whether a customer request or team proposal fits current resources and expertise, or needs more. That judgment leads to its principles:

- **Ownership:** keep team and organization levels separate. Team changes carry less risk and need smaller budgets; organization changes need customer budget and approval.
- **Depth:** build concepts first, deep enough to connect business needs with technical approaches. A clear foundation, not a shallow overview, lets managers identify costs and risks.
