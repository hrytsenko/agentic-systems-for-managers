# Agent instructions

## Vision

A lean guide for managers to talk about agents and bring them into delivery. It keeps team and organization levels separate, so managers can judge what fits an engagement and what needs customer budget and approval.

## Prose

Write for managers, not engineers. Explain concepts deep enough to connect business needs with technical approaches, following the depth principle.

## Structure

Each module is split into topics, one page each, such as agents or platforms in AI Literacy. Each topic is self-sufficient: a manager can read it alone and in any order, so topics do not link to each other, and navigation comes from the sidebar.

An AI Literacy topic opens with an intro that defines the topic and lists its core concepts as a numbered list, usually three, each saying in a few sentences what the concept is and does. Short paragraphs after the list connect the concepts to each other or to other topics, such as the harness levels.

A single Remember tip (`:::tip[Remember]`) closes the intro. It is a short checklist of what to always keep in mind, not a summary, so it need not cover every concept: up to five takeaways, each a short bold title followed by one short sentence, ordered by weight, the most important first. Readers may stop after the intro, so each takeaway stands on its own, without terms that only the sections explain.

Sections follow the intro, one per core concept and in the same order, though not every concept needs one. A section goes deeper than the intro: the parts the concept works with, how they work, and what they cost. It splits into subsections only when each part needs more than a list item. Sections have no tips of their own.

Within a topic, each term is defined once, where it first belongs, and later mentions use the term or link to it on the same page, so concepts stay lean and do not repeat each other. When a topic needs a term that another topic explains in depth, it explains the term in a clause or a sentence, enough for the point at hand, and leaves the depth to the other topic.
